# Recording Collaborative Research

These rules apply wherever an assisted phase records its collaborative exploration with the owner.

- **Recording trigger.** When a thread of exploration settles — a question or topic is resolved — append the distilled entry to the phase's recording section before moving to the next thread. The recorded unit is a distilled per-settled-thread entry capturing the exploration and its outcome, not a transcript of every reply.
- **Advocate vs. record.** The phase's "don't do the next phase's job" rule constrains advocating for or committing to a choice that belongs to a later phase. Design-adjacent (or plan-adjacent) exploration that arose collaboratively with the owner is recorded, not discarded.
