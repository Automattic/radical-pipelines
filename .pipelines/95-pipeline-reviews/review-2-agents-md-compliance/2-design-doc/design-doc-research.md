# Design Research: AGENTS.md compliance pass over PR #106 prose

The spec (`1-spec/spec.md`) defines nine concrete edit requirements (findings 1–5 plus 6a/6b/6c) and four cross-cutting constraints (no meaning lost, no new violations, delivery on the existing branch, manual verification). The design decisions below pin the exact rewrites: which statement of each duplicated fact survives and where, the precise replacement wording, and how meaning is preserved.

### Reading paths into fork-pipeline.md (Topic 1)

Four references route a reader to `fork-pipeline.md`, and none conveys "forks inherit from `base/` only / reviews not inherited" earlier in the path: `work-on-an-issue.md:31` (menu route), `review-pipeline.md:11` (precondition-fail steer), `review-pipeline.md:20` (fork-vs-review advisory), `resume-pipeline.md:34` (decline-rollback fallback). So `fork-pipeline.md` is the canonical home for the fact and the dedup is purely within this one file.

`pipeline-versioning.md:65` ("forks inherit from `base/`") is NOT on the fork-running reading path: it lives in "Deriving lineage from artifact content", and `fork-pipeline.md`'s five references into `pipeline-versioning.md` are all to other sections ("Reconstructing the pipeline tree", "Listing pipelines for an issue", "Model", "Runs within a pipeline", "Per-phase completion"). Finding 1 (within `fork-pipeline.md`) and finding 3 (within `pipeline-versioning.md`) therefore do not interact.

Negative-phrasing assessment: an explicit "reviews are never inherited" clause is not strictly necessary under `AGENTS.md:9` — every copy operation in step 5 is explicitly scoped to `base/` (the lead sentence "Copy only the phase folders being inherited, from the parent's `base/` run…" plus the `cp -r …/base/<phase>` paths), and no other fork step enumerates or walks the parent's runs, so the orchestrator has no default path that reaches `review-*` folders. A positive-only survivor satisfies both the no-duplication rule and the negative-phrasing rule at once. (Source: design-doc-researcher, grep of `skills/`, `README.md`, `AGENTS.md`.)

### Section entry points inside pipeline-versioning.md (Topic 3)

External entry points are asymmetric: "Reconstructing the pipeline tree" (lines 77–88, home of line 87) is cited directly by `work-on-an-issue.md:19` and `fork-pipeline.md:9`, while "Deriving lineage from artifact content" (lines 57–66, home of line 65) and "Rendering" (lines 89–115) are cited by no external file. So a tree-reconstructing reader can enter at line 77 and read forward through Rendering without ever passing line 65 — the downstream duplicate sites have their own entry that bypasses the canonical statement. "Rendering" is the back half of the tree-reconstruction flow, so every line-115 reader has already read line 87; no path reaches 115 without 87.

Consequence: the finding-3 dedup cannot be justified as "line 65 covers 87/115 on the reading path." It must instead be: the bare tree-membership assertion survives only at line 65 (with its causal rationale), and lines 87/115 keep their distinct positive operational instructions — trie construction from `base/` (87) and run-chain rendering (115 sentence 1) — which independently suffice for a bypassing reader to build and render the tree correctly (tree-building step 2 at line 82 already scopes SHAs to "each phase folder of its `base/` run"; the example at lines 95–105 shows no run chains). The rationale at 65 is explanatory, not operational, so a bypassing reader loses nothing operational.

Scope note: line 115 sentence 1's "not as tree nodes" contrast gloss is NOT one of finding 3's three sites (the spec quotes sentence 2, "reviews never add or move nodes") — stripping it would be an edit beyond the named sites, barred by the spec's Out of Scope. It stays. (Source: design-doc-researcher, grep + section map of `pipeline-versioning.md`.)

### Topic 1: fork-pipeline.md — single statement of "fork seeds only from `base/`; reviews not inherited"

- **Spec link:** Requirement 1 / Acceptance criterion 1 (plus cross-cutting requirements 10–11)
- **Problem:** The fact is stated three times: `fork-pipeline.md:34` (step 4, "a fork starts a fresh `base/` and never inherits the parent's reviews"), `fork-pipeline.md:38` (step 5, "Only `base/` is copied; the parent's `review-*` runs (if any) are never inherited"), and `fork-pipeline.md:43` (the parenthetical "(from the parent's `base/` run)"). The step-5 `cp -r` paths are operational and must stay complete.
- **Options:**
  1. Survivor in step 4, rewritten positively; step 5's second sentence and line 43's parenthetical cut.
  2. Survivor carried by step 5's operational "Copy only … from the parent's `base/` run" sentence; step 4's trailing clause trimmed.
- **Trade-offs:** Both pass requirements 10 and 11 (researcher-verified). Option 1 keeps the fact at the conceptual point (step 4, where the fork's artifact folder and run model are introduced) and leaves step 5 purely operational; option 2 is marginally more minimalist but leaves step 4 as a forward-pointer rather than a stated fact, and the fact then lives only inside an operational sentence.
- **Decision:** Option 1, with the surviving statement folded positively into step 4's existing sentence. Exact edits:
  - `fork-pipeline.md:34` (step 4) — replace the full second sentence with: "The fork's phases live under its own fresh `base/` run, seeded only from the parent's `base/` run in the next step (see **Runs within a pipeline** in `pipeline-versioning.md`)." This merges the former trailing clause's two facts (fresh `base/`; nothing but the parent's `base/` is inherited) into the existing clause as "fresh" and "only", with no negative phrasing and no second statement.
  - `fork-pipeline.md:38` (step 5) — delete the second sentence "Only `base/` is copied; the parent's `review-*` runs (if any) are never inherited." The lead sentence ("Copy only the phase folders being inherited, from the parent's `base/` run into the new pipeline's `base/` run — `base/0-intent` up to and including the inherited phase agreed in step 1.") and the `cp -r` bullets stay verbatim — they are the operational copy instructions the spec carves out.
  - `fork-pipeline.md:43` — delete the parenthetical "(from the parent's `base/` run)", leaving "…create a temporary worktree of the parent branch per the **Worktrees** convention, copy as above, then remove it." Line 42's in-line "in the parent's `base/` run" plus the `cp -r …/base/<phase>` source path remain the operational anchor in both bullets.
- **Rationale:** The researcher confirmed no upstream file on the reading path states the fact, so it must survive inside `fork-pipeline.md`; step 4 is where a reader first meets the fork's run layout, making it the natural single home. The positive "fresh … seeded only from" wording conveys "reviews are not inherited" without a negative clause, which the researcher's path analysis shows is unnecessary under `AGENTS.md:9` (no fork step ever reaches `review-*` folders by default). Meaning check (requirement 10): a whole-file reader still learns the fact at step 4, and the copy operation remains unambiguous from step 5's own wording.

Option 1 keeps assertion+rationale together at their canonical home; the researcher verified the bypassing reader (who enters at line 77 and never sees line 65) still builds and renders correctly from the surviving positive instructions at 87/115 plus the example.

  - `pipeline-versioning.md:65` — unchanged. ### Topic 9: Verification design

- **Spec link:** Requirements 12–13 / Acceptance criteria 10–11
- **Problem:** No automated prose oracle exists; verification is a manual passage-by-passage re-check against the five AGENTS.md rules plus the Changeset Gate CI staying green. The design specifies the checklist shape for the plan phase.
- **Options:** None — the spec fixes the mechanism (manual re-check + existing CI); the design only structures it. Decided on evidence already gathered in Topics 1–8, with no further research needed.
- **Decision:** Verification is a three-layer manual check, run after all edits land:
  1. **Per-site checks** — one check per acceptance criterion 1–9, each confirming at the edited file that (a) the flagged fact now appears exactly once in its reading path (or the flagged sentence is gone), and (b) the surviving wording is exactly the wording decided in Topics 1–8. The acceptance criteria map one-to-one onto the topic decisions, so the plan phase can lift the checklist directly from this record's "Exact edits" entries.
  2. **Cross-cutting re-read (AC 10)** — a full re-read of the PR #106 prose diff confirming requirement 10's fact enumeration is still conveyed (each fact once or via an in-path reference) and requirement 11 holds: no new duplication, negative phrasing, or non-minimal wording anywhere, and the untouchable already-compliant patterns are byte-identical — the `4 - code.md:35` / `5 - docs.md:36` parentheticals, the `intent-format.md` dedup structure, and the **Reviewer base ref** rule at `pipeline-versioning.md:21–28`.
  3. **CI (AC 11)** — commit on PR #106's existing branch (a review reuses the branch; no new pipeline, branch, or changeset), then the Changeset Gate (`npm ci`, `npm test`, `validate-changesets`, `changeset status`) passes. This is guaranteed structurally: `scripts/test/**` is untouched and the changeset front matter is byte-identical (Topic 8); only prose bodies change.
- **Rationale:** Requirement 13 says verification is manual because the repo has no markdownlint/prettier/remark/vale/textlint; the only PR-time gate is the Changeset Gate. Structuring the manual check as per-site + cross-cutting + CI gives the code phase an executable checklist whose per-site half is mechanically derivable from this document, and isolates the only judgment-heavy step (the requirement-10/11 re-read) into one explicit pass.

## Risks

- **Step 6's citation does not reach the version-unchanged justification.** After deleting step 6's second sentence, `review-pipeline.md`'s "Model" citation leads only to the v1/v2/v<N> labeling scheme (`pipeline-versioning.md:5–11`); the "a run does not change the pipeline version" fact lives at `pipeline-versioning.md:19` ("Runs within a pipeline"). Accepted because the imperative ("Re-assert (confirm, do not change) the existing `v<N>`") is executable without the why — but the design-doc reviewer should be aware this was weighed, not missed.
- **The compliance bar is judgment-based.** With no automated prose oracle, AC 10's "no new violations" rests on a manual re-read; the per-site exact-wording decisions in this record are the mitigations — the code phase should apply them verbatim rather than re-paraphrasing, since fresh paraphrases would reopen the minimalism/duplication questions each topic already settled.
- **Two files receive edits from multiple topics.** `review-pipeline.md` (Topics 2, 6, 7) and `pipeline-versioning.md` (Topics 3, 4) accumulate several independent edits; line numbers cited here are pre-edit and will shift as edits land. The plan phase should sequence or batch per-file edits and re-anchor on the quoted text, not the line numbers.
