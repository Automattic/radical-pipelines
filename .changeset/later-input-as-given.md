Hold later input to what the owner gave. A constraint, proposal, or reported failure records the owner's statement at the scope they gave it, and the evidence they pointed to, in the fewest words that carry it. A cause, a reading of the evidence, an affected artifact or clause, or a remedy the owner did not state is left to the target's producer and reviewer. A reported failure states the result as the owner put it and points to its evidence: the output the owner gave, saved beside it, and the paths that output names. Outside the assisted workflow, agents do every investigation — of code, evidence, the running software, or its environment. Triage decides from the request, the pipelines' phase-0 files, and `rp check`, and leaves what the request points to for the agents it dispatches. Its one confirmation message shows the text of every intent, constraint, proposal, or failure it will write, and asks only the questions its own steps leave open. The later-input format states the grammar of its fixed lines: `target:` paths are relative to the pipeline folder, and `origin:` is `issue <canonical reference>`, the URL of the source, or the path of the claim it answers.
