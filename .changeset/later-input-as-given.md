Hold later input to what the owner gave. A constraint, proposal, or reported failure records the owner's statement at the scope they gave it, and the evidence they pointed to, in the fewest words that carry it. A cause, a reading of the evidence, an affected artifact or clause, or a remedy the owner did not state is left to the target's producer and reviewer; a reported failure states the result as the owner put it, and the detail stays in its evidence. At triage the orchestrator judges content only to route, and `origin:` names the issue, or the URL or pipeline path the input came from.
