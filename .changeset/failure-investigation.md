A failed task report opens an investigation. While a challenge on an artifact is a failed task report or leads to one through its `origin` chain, `rp check` marks that artifact's converge and review-wave lines `(experiment)`, and their producers, reviewers, and helpers run under `Execution: experiment`. This applies in every phase. The producer establishes the failure's cause before its disposition: it reproduces the failure, checks the report's account against the raw evidence, and discriminates the candidate causes with experiments; experiments that change code go to helpers, and the producer judges what they return. Reviewers check that investigation and may run their own experiments on the failure. Experiment evidence supports only that failure's disposition. When the cause lies in delivered code, a helper proves the fix on the path that failed, and the corrective task names the obligation and carries the proven patch as its reference. Helpers under `experiment` get their own experiment seat: a worktree and branch the orchestrator creates, provisions through the worktree hooks, and removes when it lands the requester's work, after copying the helper's supporting folder into the requester's. A failed report may carry the worker's observations toward the cause, and the worker copies into its supporting folder the raw evidence the failure leaves outside the worktree. Prompts are exactly their filled templates. The orchestrator messages an agent only in reply to it or when something it owns changes for it, and a message carries only what the orchestrator owns — the seat and materials, permissions, dispatch state and records, check results — plus the next step; everything about the work reaches agents through materials or helpers.
