Triggers target what their evidence contradicts: a claim targets a clause, a failed report its task, and an external amendment one or more artifacts at the level the request states them — a clause it names, else the whole artifact — with state per target artifact and the trigger resolved when every target is. Only triggers carry target fields; `rp stamp` and `rp check` validate targets and their landing identities before computing state. Reviewers corroborate a contradicts-input disposition by the kind of evidence it carries, and one left neither corroborated nor defeated is a must-fix.
