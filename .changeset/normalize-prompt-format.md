Normalize the initial prompt into a single canonical format during pipeline creation. The phase 0 step now synthesizes the issue body, every comment, and one-hop cited references into the canonical issue format — a Title plus Goal, Constraints, Context, and Assumptions / directions to explore sections, with empty sections omitted and only Goal always present — and pins it to a documented prompt-file rendering: a top `# Prompt` heading, a `> Source:` blockquote pointing at the originating issue and noting the file is self-contained, then the canonical body sections as `## ` headings. Synthesis normalizes rather than converges, preserving the participants' stated intent without adding requirements, acceptance criteria, technical direction, or a substituted goal; hypotheses and proposed directions are filed under Assumptions, and when the body and comments conflict or a later comment revises the ask, the orchestrator reflects its best current reading and surfaces the conflict to the owner. A required owner-confirmation gate shows the exact rendered `prompt.md` text — with a revise loop for requested changes — and nothing is written or committed until the owner explicitly approves. Supporting capability grants make this possible: the `Issues` convention in setup now covers reading an issue's comments, and this repository's dogfood `.rp.md` documents the `Reading an issue` step.
