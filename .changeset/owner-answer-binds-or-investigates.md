Let the owner answer an escalation with a proposal as well as a constraint: the orchestrator asks whether each item of incoming owner input binds or is open to investigation, records rulings at the scope the owner gave them, and `rp check` resolves a claim answered by either file. An answer replaces the challenged obligation within its targets: a constraint binds them; a proposal they investigate and adopt or refute.
