Let the owner answer an escalation with a proposal as well as a constraint: the orchestrator asks whether each item of incoming owner input binds or is open to investigation, records rulings at the scope the owner gave them, and `rp check` resolves a claim answered by either file. An answer replaces the challenged obligation within its targets — as binding work for a constraint, as a direction the targets adopt or refute for a proposal.
