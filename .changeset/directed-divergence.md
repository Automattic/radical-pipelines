Direct the divergence in the design-doc phase's divergent mode. Each lane works a positional mandate — the first designs from the spec alone, each subsequent lane differs from the previous designs in at least one load-bearing decision, and the last breaks an assumption they all share — with optional owner-named lane angles replacing the defaults. The lane declares its divergence as a decision in its record — what the previous designs chose, what its design chooses, and why that serves its mandate — putting the divergence itself under the evidence contract and the lane's adversarial review.
