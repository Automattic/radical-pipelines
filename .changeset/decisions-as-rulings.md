Record a decision as the ruling, approved by the owner, with its origin, instead of the owner's words quoted verbatim; append it only when it changes what the work must satisfy. Every other intent item is already the orchestrator's synthesis written on approval; verbatim quoting was a second guard on one section only, and an answer given as an option label put a token in the permanent record that means nothing without the question it answered. The origin keeps the trail to where the ruling was given, which the quote only carried indirectly. A ruling that leaves the work as it stands authorises nothing and no record cites it.
