Hold the test surface to what the outcomes need. An acceptance criterion states the input, where the outcome is observed, and the outcome, never the fixtures, sequences, or variants that prove it; the spec reviewer reports a criterion that carries them. The proof and test economy rule names its unit: proofs, tests, cases, and parameter rows that exercise the same mechanism with the same kind of input are one proof, and one that cannot fail is removed or reported. A reason for rejecting an alternative is a claim judged under the claims rules, no longer a proof the build ships. Specs were writing criteria as test scripts, every later phase was faithful to them, two tests of one mechanism counted as different because they served different criteria, and each rejected alternative shipped a test of its own.
