Apply the materiality rule when a finding is adjudicated. Before disposing of a finding that adds a case, mechanism, guard, or proof, the spec, design-doc, and build-plan producers name the intended use that produces its input; when none does, the finding is refuted, or recorded as contradicting the input whose clause demands the case. Producers were adopting every finding that was true of the mechanism, so a design grew to handle inputs nothing produces and each absorbed case shipped its own test; the rule that a finding must justify why its case matters was applied to the finding's wording, never to the case.
