Keep runs moving on questions the intent leaves open. A producer follows the intent's words where they decide a question; where they leave it open, it takes the reading the Goal and intended use support and records the other reading in the artifact — the spec's Considered and not required, or the decision's Alternatives — where the owner can correct it. A thread to `owner` is opened only for a disagreement between roles on what the intent means or demands, or for evidence that every kind of approach fails an intent goal or constraint, and its `cites` names that thread or evidence; `rp check` reports a thread to `owner` without one as malformed. A production lane decides every question by the same rules as any producer, its candidate holding only its final reading; it opens no thread, `rp check` reports a thread with a production-lane party as malformed, and its producer resolves where candidates differ when it consolidates them. A candidate's required inputs are only what its lane reads — the intent for a spec candidate, the spec for a design doc candidate — so a later input targeting the spec or design doc goes to the producer when it consolidates and reruns no lane. Every role ends its turn only after every command and subagent it started has finished and it has acted on the result.
