Keep runs moving on questions the intent leaves open. A producer follows the intent's words where they decide a question; where they leave it open, it takes the reading the Goal and intended use support and records the other reading in the artifact — the spec's Considered and not required, or the decision's Alternatives — where the owner can correct it. A thread to `owner` is opened only for a disagreement between roles on what the intent means or demands, or for evidence that every kind of approach fails an intent goal or constraint, and carries a `cites:` header line naming that thread or evidence; `rp check` reports a thread to `owner` without one as `INVALID`. A production lane decides every question by the same rules as any producer, its candidate holding only its final reading; it opens no thread, and the consolidator resolves where lanes differ. `rp check` reports an `open` or `disagreement` thread with a production-lane party as `INVALID` and no longer routes threads to lanes; closed lane threads from earlier runs stay readable. A candidate's required inputs are only what its lane reads — the intent for a spec candidate, the spec for a design doc candidate — so a later input targeting the spec or design doc goes to the consolidator and reruns no lane. Every role ends its turn only after every command and subagent it started has finished and it has acted on the result. The thread format in every profile lists the artifact values again. The message that wakes a role only points to its assignment; the turn commit lives only in the assignment, where the orchestrator reads it at landing.
