Have roles argue in threads instead of adopting what they are told. A finding or an answer may rest on a judgment — a role's opinion that a case matters, is out of scope, is disproportionate to the intent, or has a better alternative — stated with its reason, as well as on observed evidence; a claim about what the software does is still observed. A role opens a thread when it disagrees with an artifact it consumes, not only when it finds a problem in it. The owner of an artifact judges every point before answering: it changes the artifact for one that convinces it and answers that the artifact stands for one that does not; this replaces the rule that judged only findings adding a case, whose trace to the intent or to intended use is now what makes such a finding convincing. The opener judges each answer the same way, dropping a finding whose answer convinces it and holding one whose answer does not, and the reviewer's later-turn request says so. The implementer adds its own judgment to a reviewer's finding it carries to a clause's owner. A disagreement is a reply that repeats a position without new evidence or a new argument, and one on the intent's scope or on what is proportionate to it goes to the owner, as any disagreement on what the intent means or demands.
