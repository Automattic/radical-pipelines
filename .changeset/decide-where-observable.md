BREAKING: Each phase binds at its boundaries and leaves the inside to the phase that observes it. The design doc decides contracts — signatures, schemas, the behavior a caller observes — and a decision that supplies a component's control flow, call sequences, names, or test cases, in prose or in code, is a finding. A build task names its boundary and the outcomes that must hold there; the shape inside is the worker's, whether the task would supply it directly or through a passage it cites, and a corrective task names the obligation and established cause of a failure without carrying a fix: the `Reference` field is gone. A document task names surfaces, an audience, and what the reader leaves with; one task covers a feature's documentation for one audience across every surface that audience reads, and what a surface states is the writer's. Reviewers' fidelity checks ask whether two implementers would build to the same contracts, satisfy the same acceptance, or cover the same surfaces, not produce the same text. An obligation an agent chose is that agent's to revise: adopting a finding may simplify or remove a mechanism, Proportion is a finding in the design, build plan, and build reviews — a mechanism, guard, or proof that serves no requirement, criterion, or constraint — an assumption whose circumstance no material input produces is carried rather than verified, and a build reviewer treats code that satisfies a clause the running software shows wrong as a contradiction of that clause.

Across sixteen audited runs, upstream artifacts pre-made the downstream decisions: the design doc held the implementation and the worker typed it; the document plan held the facts and the writer phrased them. Decisions were made by agents who could only inspect and checked by agents who could only compare to the design, so a wrong upstream decision shipped as "decided", and every caught worker mistake became upstream precision that nothing ever reversed.
