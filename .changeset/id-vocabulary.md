BREAKING: one id vocabulary across every artifact — `<prefix>-<word>-<n>`, the prefix naming the artifact class that originates the id and the word its concept: `intent-goal`, `intent-constraint-n`, `intent-context-n`, `intent-assumption-n`, `intent-decision-n`; `spec-requirement-n`, `spec-acceptance-criterion-n`, `spec-assumption-n`; `design-doc-decision-n`, `design-doc-assumption-n`; `build-assumption-n` and `build-task-n` (files `3-build/tasks/build-task-n.md`); `document-assumption-n` and `document-task-n`; `<phase>-finding-n` in reviews; `<phase>-question-n` in records. An item opens with its id, inline formatting marks aside; an assumption keeps its id downstream. The intent, spec, design doc, and plans mirror their declared ids as `ids` and `retired-ids` (replacing `intent-ids`), validated at their own stamp: declared once, never reused, numbered without gaps from 1, a carried assumption declared by the upstream artifact; a violation is `INVALID IDS`, which the file's author fixes. A review's must-fix entries live under `## Findings`.
