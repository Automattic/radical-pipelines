Hold the document phase to a prose standard: content is what the audience acts on, at the depth they act on it, in the voice and vocabulary of the surrounding document; within a surface, each fact is explained once and referenced from the rest; every surface describes the software as it is, and the change only where the change is the subject. The worker and reviewer judge a behavior claim as the audience follows it within supported use, so an exception reached only outside it is not falsity; the reviewer treats a second explanation of a fact, a mechanism the reader cannot act on, and a term foreign to the audience as findings. The plan explains each fact once, a task dictating sentences is a finding whatever their source, and the build and document plan reviewers reject for a breach of any rule under **Rules**, as the phase reviewers already did. Remove the `Documentation conventions` material from the document worker and reviewer: no convention defined it, and a guardrail targeting those profiles delivers a project's documentation guides. Corrective waves on documentation were only ever adding: each finding became a qualifier appended at every site that mentioned the fact, in the vocabulary of the artifacts, until the surface no longer read as documentation.
