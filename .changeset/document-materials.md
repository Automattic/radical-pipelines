Base documentation planning on the intent and the net change, using the spec and the design doc to settle what changed. Have document workers state, per readership and in two or three sentences, what readers can now do, must now do, or no longer need to do, check it against the spec and the design doc, and record why each edit is needed; have document reviewers check that statement against the same inputs, treat behavior the change leaves as it was as no reader outcome, and flag passages no reader outcome requires for removal. Check document plans against the planner's ownership boundary before declaring them ready.
