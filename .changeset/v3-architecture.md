BREAKING: Architecture v3. A pipeline is a converging set of artifacts: state is computed from the working tree (body-hash identities, pins in frontmatter written only by the orchestrator's `rp stamp`, `rp check` reporting the frontier), corrections are amendments that cascade through staleness instead of forks and revisions, and every artifact loop carries a third verdict — `unsatisfiable` — that routes a contradiction to the artifact that must change, up to the owner when it contradicts a recorded owner statement. Spec and design phases verify by inspection only and label what they cannot verify as assumptions that build verifies first. Plans are folders of task files; task reports pin the tasks they executed. Profiles are rewritten on one schema (Role, Seat, Modes, Rules, Protocol, Formats) with prompt templates in the skill; consolidators become a producer mode; writers become workers; research goes through one `researcher`; phase summaries are gone. An `Agents` convention configures each profile's model and its named lanes — production lanes with a brief, optionally `after` others; review lanes with a brief and materials — replacing `Agent models`. Tool mechanics move from `.rp.md` into the skill's `tools/` files; `.rp.md` gains a `conventions` schema stamp with a migration changelog. Blockers only report malformed materials or a broken environment. The opencode plugin removes retired profiles when it materializes agents and refuses to spawn a foreign profile colliding with an RP one.
